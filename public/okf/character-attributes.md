# The 5 Core Real-World Character Attributes

## 1. Architectural Philosophy

Traditional task managers treat all tasks as homogeneous checklist items: answering an email, bench pressing 200 pounds, and meditating for 20 minutes look visually identical. This creates cognitive flattening, where users gravitate toward trivial administrative tasks to check boxes rather than undertaking high-value deep work or physical health habits.

Life RPG introduces **Character Attribute Alignment**: every task feeds one of five distinct attributes, providing immediate feedback on whether a user's life is balanced or skewed.

```
       [INTELLECT]
       /         \
 [SPIRIT]       [STRENGTH]
     \             /
   [VITALITY] - [AGILITY]
```

## 2. Attribute Specifications

### 2.1 Intellect (INT)
- **Domain**: Cognitive stamina, academic learning, synthesis, and deep analytical problem-solving.
- **Example Real-Life Tasks**: Reading non-fiction book chapters, coding complex software features, practicing algorithmic problem sets, studying language grammar, scientific writing.
- **In-Game Function**: Boosts maximum Focus Sanctuary Pomodoro XP yields and unlocks academic titles in the Guild Codex.

### 2.2 Strength (STR)
- **Domain**: Physical resistance, muscular hypertrophy, grit, and heavy manual exertion.
- **Example Real-Life Tasks**: Weightlifting (squat, bench, deadlift), calisthenics, rock climbing, high-intensity physical labor, moving heavy furniture.
- **In-Game Function**: Amplifies damage dealt per quest completion against the Sloth Behemoth raid boss.

### 2.3 Agility (AGI)
- **Domain**: Cardiovascular endurance, speed of task execution, mobility, and quick habit transitions.
- **Example Real-Life Tasks**: Interval sprint training, 5K/10K running, dynamic yoga/stretching routines, quick 10-minute domestic cleanup sweeps, rapid inbox zero sprints.
- **In-Game Function**: Decreases cooldown timers on daily challenge refreshes and grants bonus Bounty Gold (GP) on rapid completions.

### 2.4 Vitality (VIT)
- **Domain**: Physiological recovery, immune resilience, biological maintenance, and sleep hygiene.
- **Example Real-Life Tasks**: Achieving 8 hours of sleep, drinking 2.5L of water daily, cooking a balanced whole-food meal, taking daily vitamins, taking a recovery walk.
- **In-Game Function**: Increases maximum player Hit Points (HP), protecting your character's streak shield from degradation on unavoidable rest days.

### 2.5 Spirit (SPI)
- **Domain**: Emotional regulation, mindfulness, existential clarity, artistic creativity, and gratitude.
- **Example Real-Life Tasks**: Daily meditation (10-20 minutes), journaling, drawing/painting, playing an acoustic instrument, expressing gratitude to friends or family, digital detox sessions.
- **In-Game Function**: Elevates Lumi's ambient mood states and unlocks rare companion cosmetic auras in the Guild Emporium.

## 3. The Radar Chart & Life Balance Index

In the Character Codex tab (`/?tab=codex`), user attribute scores are dynamically rendered via an interactive SVG radar pentagram. 

A user with 80 Intellect but 5 Vitality receives visual diagnostic alerts ("Vitality Deficit Warning"), nudging them toward restorative habits rather than continuous burnout-inducing cognitive marathons.
